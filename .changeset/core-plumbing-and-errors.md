`requestWithCredential` makes a request with the `Authorization` header that the host's `authorization` gives for it, under the signals of the call, and refuses a value that could end the header; `CredentialServiceCall` is its service, and `clientOptionsEntries` holds the schemas of the options that every client of a service takes. `@interopkit/core/checks` gains `readCallOptions`, which reads the `signal` of the options of a call, `checkBaseUrl`, which refuses an address of a service with credentials, a query or a fragment, `formatDateTime`, which writes whole epoch milliseconds as RFC 3339 in UTC with milliseconds, and `Issue`, a finding with its `severity`, which every validator of the family lists. `readAnswer` keeps what the reader threw as the `cause` of its `DataError`, and the signer, `signCompactJws` and the keyed cache refuse options of a call that are not an object.

`LimitError`, a `DataError` with `limit`, the name of the limit, and `maximum`, its value, is what every reader of the family throws for input over a limit, so that a host answers 413 from one check: `readJson` and `readForm` of `@interopkit/core/http` at `maxBytes`, `maxDepth` and `maxTokens`, `readAnswerJson` at `maxDepth` and `maxTokens`, and the key set cache at 100 keys; `readAnswer` keeps a `LimitError` of its reader as one. Every error class takes `ErrorOptions`, `ServiceErrorOptions` extends it, and an error that reports another carries it as its `cause`: the failure of a `fetch` and its deadline, the refusals of oauth4webapi, and the errors of a decoder and of `JSON.parse`.

`AccessRefusal` names its cause `reason`, a term of the exported kebab-case union `AccessRefusalReason`: `unauthenticated`, `forbidden` or `too-many-requests`, which sets the status of the answer, 401, 403 or 429, as `ACCESS_REFUSAL_STATUSES` writes it; the sentence of the answer is `message`, and `status` is gone, so that a refusal is `{ reason, message }` as every refusal of the family is.

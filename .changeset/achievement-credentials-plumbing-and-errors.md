A finding is an `Issue` of `@interopkit/core/checks`, which replaces `CredentialIssue`. The client of the Open Badges and CLR APIs makes its requests through `requestWithCredential` of core, and refuses a `baseUrl` with an empty query or fragment, as it refuses one with a query or a fragment.

The limits of the readers throw `LimitError` of core: the bytes and the tokens of a baked image and its credential, the levels and the attributes of an SVG, the tokens of a context, of a refreshed credential and of an answer of the client, and `maxJsonLdTokens`, the levels and the runs of Hash N-Degree Quads when signing, deriving and validating. The provider answers 413 for JSON over `maxTokens` or 64 levels, in a body or in the payload of a JWS, where it answered 400 or 422.

A finding of a course structure or of the statements of a session is an `Issue` of `@interopkit/core/checks`, which replaces `CourseIssue`. A call of an assignable unit refuses options that are not an object.

The limits of the readers of a course structure and of a package, `maxBytes`, `maxDepth`, `maxElements`, `maxEntries` and `maxDirectoryBytes`, and those of `readLaunchUrl`, 65,536 characters and 1,000 tokens of the actor, throw `LimitError` of core.

A finding of `validateEvent` and `validateEntity` is an `Issue` of `@interopkit/core/checks`, which replaces `CaliperFinding`, and a time is written by `formatDateTime` of `@interopkit/core/checks`, which replaces the `formatDateTime` of this package: it takes no label, refuses a fraction of a millisecond, and writes the years 0000 to 1969 too, which the readers of Caliper accept.

The endpoint answers 413 for JSON over its levels or `maxTokens`, where it answered 400; `sendCaliper` throws `LimitError` of core for an envelope over `maxPayloadBytes`, and `pingEndpoint` for an answer that nests more than 64 levels.
